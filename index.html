How It Works

Step 1: Open Your Project

Launch Linguan and select your Xcode project. It scans all strings files instantly.

Step 2: Identify Gaps

Review warnings for duplicates or missing translations. Filter by language as needed.

Step 3: Export and Collaborate

Export tokens for a specific language and send to your translator via email.

Step 4: Merge Results

Import the completed file. Linguan merges translations automatically into your project.
